      How it works

      1. You describe what you want. The request is turned into an industry code and a few search words.
      2. We search the Finnish Trade Register for active limited companies in that industry.
      3. We read each company’s own website and quote any passage that mentions your search words.
      4. You pick companies and export a report with links to every source.

      Keep in mind

      A word match only means the words appear on the website. It does not mean the company fits a buyer. Revenue, ownership and willingness to sell are not in public data. “Check ownership” marks websites that mention a merger or acquisition.
